Homes

An animal that lives somewhere has somewhere to live. The rabbit has a warren, and the warren is a thing: it has a place, an owner species, a size and an age, it is in the save, the tile under it can be asked about, and a rabbit that finds none of its own builds one. Burrows are first. Nests and mounds come next on the same list.

Source: src/sim/homes/homes.ts, src/sim/animals/animals.ts, src/render/lit/homePass.ts, src/render/lit/shaders/homes.vert, src/render/lit/shaders/homes.frag

1A home is an entity

  1. PlaceNine numbers on a packed list (Homes in sim/homes/homes.ts, up to 4096): kind (burrow, nest, ant hill, termite mound), the species that owns it, x and y in metres, the game day it was started, the days it takes to build and how many entrances it has.
  2. SettlingWhen a group of an animal that has a home habit (dwells({ kind, days }) in its behaviour) is made, it looks for a home of its own kind and species within 14 m of where it stands and joins it, or starts a new one at that spot. A home grows an entrance for every two animals that have settled in it, at most eight. **A warren is not one mound**: each entrance is its own hole at its own place round the home's centre (on a spiral a few metres across, worked out from the home's id, so nothing is stored per entrance) and they are joined underground, so a big warren is a scatter of small dirt heaps, and each is dug from the day it is added.
  3. BuildingA home is not finished when it is started: its progress is the game days since then over its build days, worked out on demand, so it keeps on whether anyone is looking or not. Below 0.55 it is a scrape of loosened earth, then the hole opens and the mound rises. An animal that is startled runs for its home and hides only if it is built, otherwise it just runs.
  4. Saved and mirroredThe list is in the save (WorldState.homes) and goes to the main thread with the world delta whenever it changes, like the fires and the plans.

Not done yet: nests (a bird builds in a tree for days before the eggs), ant hills and termite mounds (still drawn from the habitat, not from a home), a home limiting how many animals a patch can hold, abandoning and digging out, the tile sheet and what people can do to a home.

2How it is drawn

An entrance is a model like any animal (src/animals/burrow/, the home group): a rubbly apron of spoil with a small terraced dome of soil on it, drawn once for each entrance of a warren by a pass of its own (HomePass, homes.vert and homes.frag) in the same three-quarter view as the mammals and snapped to the pixel grid. The entrance's own build progress is the model's grow: below 0.15 only the scrape, then the dome rises in three steps, and from half built it has a dark hole with a rim on its slope. The mammal pass no longer draws its own mound in the game (the sheet and the zoo, which have no world, still do). npm run animal -- homes draws a chart of built against entrances; the nest, the ant hill and the termite mound will be a model each.
