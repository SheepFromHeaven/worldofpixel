How the world works

One page per system. Every graph and every map on these pages is drawn by running the game's own code, so what you read here is what the simulation does. Regenerate with npm run wiki after changing a system and the pictures follow.

World generationFrom a seed to 3.3 million tiles: height, sea, rain, rivers, climate and biomes.GeologyRocks, strata, minerals and caves under every tile, from the plates down.Bigger worldsA coarse global field, 1 m windows made on demand, geology per window, and catching up ground nobody watched.EcosystemHow well things grow, what a tile wants, water plants, the seasons and what changes the ground.PlantsThe species catalogue and encyclopedia, how plants grow, compete for light and water, spread seed and bear fruit.InsectsBees, butterflies, ants and the rest as a field of abundance, not a crowd of agents.FishShoals in rivers, lakes and the sea: what they need, where they swim, what they eat, and how they are drawn.AmphibiansFrogs, toads, newts and salamanders as a field with a life cycle: spawn, tadpoles, adults.ReptilesSnakes, lizards, chameleons, turtles and crocodilians as a field: ectotherms that bask, bolt for water and slither.BirdsSongbirds, raptors, waterfowl, sea birds and flightless runners that perch, soar, swim, dive, migrate and take off when startled.MammalsMoles to whales: herds, packs, prides, pods and families that breed, nurse, graze, hunt, dive, burrow and hibernate.The food webEvery animal eats, breeds, is eaten and grows up: insects, fish, amphibians, reptiles and birds in one balance.WeatherTemperature, pressure, wind, clouds, rain, snow, storms and lightning, all driven by Sky.Sun, moon and seasonsReal celestial geometry on a strip of planet: day length, the terminator, moon phases and hemispheres.The mindDiscovery as a tree of neurons: what people have met, the guesses they try, and the recipes that come out.MusicEleven pieces played by an oscillator synth: the score, the voices, the kit and the mix.

Adding a page

A page is one file in tools/wiki/pages/ exporting a Page: a title, a lede and a list of sections. Import the sim's functions and call them, never copy a constant, so a chart cannot drift from the code it explains. Numbers quoted in the prose are typed by hand and are the part that can go stale, so re-read them when a system changes. Register the page in tools/wiki/index.ts.
